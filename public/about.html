About Virtual Arcade

Virtual Arcade is a free, browser-based multiplayer hangout for casino-style and classic games. You land in a shared lobby, pick a game, and play with people in the same room — no download required.

Not a gambling site

We are not a real-money casino or sportsbook. There is no depositing or withdrawing cash, no prizes with monetary value from us, and no gambling with real currency. In-game chips are a virtual gameplay mechanic only for entertainment, as described in our Terms.

What You Can Play

  • Texas Hold'em (poker-style)
  • Blackjack
  • Slots
  • Checkers (human or computer)
  • Chess (human or computer)

How rooms work

You start in a default room when you open the site. The room key groups everyone together: same key means shared lobby, chat, and games. Use Switch Room to join a private code with friends, or Invite to share your link.

Accounts and progress

Play as a guest anytime, or create an account to keep your balance and cosmetic unlocks across sessions. Sign-in options are available from the Account button in the lobby.

Privacy and ads

We use the information we collect to run the games and accounts — we do not sell your personal data. If you see targeted or personalized ads, that comes from Google AdSense under Google's own policies and controls, not from us building ad profiles to sell. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
