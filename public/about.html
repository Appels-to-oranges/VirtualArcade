About Virtual Arcade

Virtual Arcade is a browser-based multiplayer game room where players can join with a room key and play quick casino and board games together.

What You Can Play

  • Texas Hold'em
  • Blackjack
  • Slots
  • Checkers (player and computer mode)
  • Chess (player and computer mode)

How Rooms Work

Enter a room key on the home screen and share that key with friends. Players using the same key join the same room and can chat, switch games, and play in real time.

Accounts and Progress

You can play as a guest, or create an account to keep your balance and cosmetic unlocks across sessions.
