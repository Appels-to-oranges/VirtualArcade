Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

What we use your information for

We process data you give us or that the service generates to operate Virtual Arcade: accounts (such as username and email when you register), gameplay-related data (such as chips and cosmetic choices), room and chat activity as needed to run sessions, and basic technical logs for security and reliability. We use this data to provide and improve the product — not to build profiles for sale to advertisers or data brokers.

We do not sell your personal data

Virtual Arcade does not sell your personal information. We do not trade your contact details or identity for money or other consideration. We also do not use your personal data to run our own targeted advertising business beyond what is described here.

Cookies, storage, and sessions

Session cookies and similar mechanisms keep you signed in and maintain game state. Local browser storage may hold preferences (for example volume or visual settings). These are for the service itself, not for sale.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google may use cookies, mobile identifiers, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized or interest-based ads where allowed, according to Google's advertising policies. Any such targeting or measurement is handled by Google's systems, not by us selling your data.

You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage ad personalization (where available) via Google's Privacy Policy and Google Ads Settings.

Contact

For privacy requests, email dev@virtualarcade.space.
