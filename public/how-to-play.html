How to Play

Get started quickly and jump into games with friends.

1) Join a Room

Enter an alias and room key, then press Enter Room. Anyone with the same room key joins your room.

2) Pick a Game

Use the game cards in the lobby to switch into Hold'em, Blackjack, Slots, Checkers, or Chess.

3) Wagers and Computer Modes

In board games, you can play against another player or choose computer mode when available. Computer mode uses fixed payouts based on selected difficulty.

4) Accounts

Guests can play immediately. Creating an account lets you keep chips and cosmetics between sessions.
